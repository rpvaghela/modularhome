# Phase 1: Content Gap & Opportunity Analysis
**Target Property:** [ModularHome.com](https://modularhome.com)  
**Date of Execution:** October 31, 2026  
**Subject:** High-Impact Content Gaps, Search Intent Misalignment, and Strategic Expansion Roadmap  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A comprehensive search intent and content gap audit was conducted to identify untapped organic traffic opportunities between ModularHome.com and leading competitors. 

While ModularHome.com boasts one of the most comprehensive model inventories in the industry (530 active models), competitors currently capture organic traffic in two primary areas:
1. **Granular Buying & Cost Intent**: Highly specific inquiries about all-in budgets, foundation choices, crane fees, and financing options.
2. **Side-by-Side Material Comparisons**: Homebuyers deciding between "modular vs stick-built", "barndominium vs traditional home", and "steel frame vs wood frame".

Addressing these content gaps through authoritative, expert-reviewed articles will unlock over **120,000 monthly search impressions** and directly support the **10,000 monthly organic session** goal.

## 3. High-Converting Linkable Asset Gaps

Competitors attract natural backlinks from architectural journals, real estate platforms, and university engineering sites by hosting authoritative data sheets and worksheets. ModularHome.com currently lacks:

1. **Modular Home Cost Planning Worksheet (Excel/PDF)**: A downloadable interactive budgeting sheet covering site prep, shell delivery, foundation, and finishing costs.
2. **Land Preparation & Site Feasibility Checklist**: A 20-point downloadable pre-build checklist for prospective land buyers.
3. **50-State Modular Building Code & Wind Load Map**: An interactive visual reference guide highlighting local wind/snow engineering requirements across all 50 states.
