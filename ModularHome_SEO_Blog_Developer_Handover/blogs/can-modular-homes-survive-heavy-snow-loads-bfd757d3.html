Why Snow Load Engineering Matters

Snow may look harmless —
but heavy snow creates:

enormous structural weight.

Wet snow especially can become EXTREMELY heavy on:

  • roofs

  • porches

  • overhangs

  • decks

That is why homes in snow-prone regions require:

specific structural engineering.

What Is a Snow Load?

Snow load refers to:

the amount of weight a roof is engineered to safely support from accumulated snow.

Different regions require different snow-load ratings depending on:

  • climate

  • elevation

  • local weather patterns

  • county code requirements
