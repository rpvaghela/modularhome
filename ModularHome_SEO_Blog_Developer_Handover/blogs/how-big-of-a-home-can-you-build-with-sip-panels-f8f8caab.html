Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/how-big-of-a-home-can-you-build-with-sip-panels

SIP construction is actually EXTREMELY flexible.

What Are SIP Panels Again?

SIP stands for:

Structural Insulated Panel.

These are engineered building panels made from:

  • rigid foam insulation
    between

  • structural sheathing layers

usually:

  • OSB panels.

SIPs become:

  • exterior walls

  • roof systems

  • floor systems

depending on the project.

So… How BIG Can a SIP Home Be?

The honest answer is:

VERY big.

There is really no “standard maximum size” for a SIP home.

SIP homes can range from:

  • tiny 200 sq ft cabins
    all the way up to:

  • 5,000+ sq ft luxury homes

  • large commercial buildings

  • multi-unit structures

when engineered properly.
