What Is an A-Frame Home?

An A-frame home is a steeply pitched triangular-style structure where the roof extends almost to the ground on both sides, forming the shape of the letter:

“A”.

The design became popular decades ago for:

  • ski cabins

  • mountain homes

  • vacation retreats

but modern architecture and social media have brought A-frames back in a HUGE way.

Today’s A-frames often combine:

  • modern glass walls

  • black exteriors

  • vaulted ceilings

  • luxury interiors

  • minimalist design

  • dramatic window systems

creating a cabin style people instantly recognize online.
