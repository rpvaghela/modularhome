What Is an ADU?

An ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) is a smaller secondary home built on the same property as a primary house.

ADUs can include:

  • detached backyard homes

  • garage apartments

  • basement apartments

  • guest houses

  • converted garages

  • tiny backyard cottages

Most ADUs range from:

  • 150 sq ft

  • up to around 1,200 sq ft

depending on local zoning rules. (Mercatus Center)

Why ADUs Are Exploding in Popularity

1. Housing Costs Are Out of Control

The #1 reason ADUs are booming is affordability.

Traditional housing prices and rents have skyrocketed across America, making it harder for:

  • young adults

  • retirees

  • middle-class families

to afford housing.

ADUs offer a cheaper way to:

  • create living space

  • generate rental income

  • house family members

  • avoid moving entirely

Many homeowners now realize:

building an ADU can be cheaper than buying a new house.

(House Beautiful)

2. Multi-Generational Living Is Growing Fast

Families increasingly want to keep:

  • aging parents

  • adult children

  • relatives

close by.

ADUs are PERFECT for:

  • in-law suites

  • retirement housing

  • family guest homes

  • adult children returning home

This trend has accelerated rapidly in recent years. (House Beautiful)

3. Airbnb Changed Everything

ADUs became massively popular after Airbnb exploded.

Homeowners realized they could:

  • build backyard cottages

  • rent them short-term

  • create passive income

  • increase property value

Modern ADUs perform EXTREMELY well on:

  • Airbnb

  • Vrbo

  • vacation rental platforms

especially when they feature:

  • modern design

  • outdoor living

  • stylish interiors

  • privacy

4. Cities Need More Housing FAST

America has a major housing shortage.

ADUs are becoming popular because they add:

  • smaller affordable housing

  • rental inventory

  • “missing middle” housing

WITHOUT requiring:

  • massive apartment complexes

  • new subdivisions

  • major land development

That is why many states are relaxing ADU laws. (Mercatus Center)

5. ADUs Increase Property Value

Many homeowners view ADUs as:

property upgrades

because they can:

  • generate rental income

  • add livable square footage

  • increase flexibility

  • improve resale appeal

This has made ADUs especially attractive in expensive housing markets. (Rudy's Construction)

6. Remote Work Changed Housing Priorities

Remote work changed how people use property.

Homeowners now want:

  • backyard offices

  • private studios

  • guest suites

  • detached workspaces

ADUs fit these new lifestyles perfectly.

7. ADUs Are More Accepted Legally Now

This is HUGE.

For years, many cities restricted ADUs heavily.

Now states and cities are:

  • streamlining permits

  • relaxing zoning

  • removing parking requirements

  • allowing detached units

This is one of the biggest reasons ADUs are exploding right now. (The Washington Post)
