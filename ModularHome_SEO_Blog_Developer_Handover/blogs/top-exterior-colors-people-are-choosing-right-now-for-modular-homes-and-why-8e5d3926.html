One of the biggest trends in modular homes right now is:

modern exterior color design.

Today’s modular homes look NOTHING like older prefab stereotypes.

Modern buyers now want modular homes that feel:

  • luxurious

  • timeless

  • modern

  • rustic

  • cozy

  • architecturally impressive

And exterior color plays a HUGE role in that.

Across America, the MOST popular modular home colors right now are:

1. Black & Matte Black

2. White & Warm Farmhouse Whites

3. Charcoal Gray & Burnished Slate

4. Sage Green & Earthy Greens

5. Natural Wood & Cedar Accents

These colors dominate because they match the biggest trends in:

Black modular homes absolutely exploded in popularity over the last several years.

Why?

Because black creates:

  • dramatic curb appeal

  • luxury aesthetics

  • modern architecture vibes

  • clean lines

  • mountain-modern style

Black homes dominate:

  • YouTube

  • Pinterest

  • TikTok

  • Instagram

because they photograph INCREDIBLY well. 

Why Buyers Love Black Modular Homes

Black exteriors make modular homes feel:

  • expensive

  • custom-designed

  • modern

  • architectural

People especially love pairing black with:

  • cedar porches

  • wood beams

  • stone accents

  • giant windows

  • black roofing

This became one of the defining looks of:

modern modular architecture.

Matte Black Became Especially Popular

Many buyers now prefer:

  • matte black

  • textured black

  • crinkle black finishes

instead of glossy finishes because they feel:

  • softer

  • richer

  • more upscale
