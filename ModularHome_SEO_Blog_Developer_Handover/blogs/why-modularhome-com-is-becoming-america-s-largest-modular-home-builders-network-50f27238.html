Why Modular Construction Is Growing So Fast

Several major trends are fueling growth across the modular housing industry:

1. Rising Housing Costs

Traditional homes have become increasingly unaffordable for many Americans. Modular construction often provides a more efficient and cost-controlled alternative.

2. Labor Shortages

Factory-built construction reduces dependency on large on-site labor crews while improving build consistency.

3. Faster Build Times

Many modular homes and prefab systems can be completed significantly faster than traditional site-built homes.

4. Increased Demand for ADUs

Accessory dwelling units, granny pods, and backyard homes are becoming increasingly popular nationwide.

5. Growth of Steel Construction

Steel house kits and steel modular homes are rapidly gaining attention due to durability, energy efficiency, and modern design flexibility.
