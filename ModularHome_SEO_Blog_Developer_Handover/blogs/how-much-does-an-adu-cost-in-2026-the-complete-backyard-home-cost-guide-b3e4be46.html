What Is an ADU?

An ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) is a secondary smaller home placed on the same property as a primary house.

Common ADU types include:

  • backyard cottages

  • detached guest houses

  • granny pods

  • garage apartments

  • prefab ADUs

  • tiny backyard homes

ADUs are commonly used for:

  • rental income

  • Airbnb units

  • family housing

  • retirement suites

  • offices

  • guest space

Why ADU Costs Vary So Much

Several major factors affect ADU pricing.

1. Utility Connections

One of the BIGGEST hidden costs is utilities.

ADUs may require:

  • electrical hookups

  • water lines

  • sewer/septic connections

  • internet

  • gas or propane

Utility work can become VERY expensive depending on:

  • distance

  • excavation

  • city requirements

2. Foundation Type

ADUs may sit on:

  • slabs

  • crawl spaces

  • piers

  • chassis systems

Permanent foundations usually increase costs but may improve:

  • financing

  • zoning approval

  • long-term value

3. Prefab vs Site-Built

Prefab ADUs are exploding because they can reduce:

  • labor costs

  • build timelines

  • weather delays

  • scheduling issues

Site-built ADUs often cost more because:

  • labor is higher

  • timelines are longer

  • construction happens entirely onsite

Prefab and modular ADUs are one of the fastest-growing housing segments today.

4. Local Permits and Impact Fees

Some cities charge:

  • permit fees

  • sewer impact fees

  • utility fees

  • zoning fees

These costs vary dramatically depending on location.

California ADU permits, for example, can become expensive in certain cities.

5. Interior Finishes

Interior upgrades heavily affect pricing.

Luxury features include:

  • quartz countertops

  • tile showers

  • custom kitchens

  • smart-home systems

  • black modern interiors

  • designer lighting

Finish quality is one of the biggest variables.

Hidden ADU Costs Most People Forget
