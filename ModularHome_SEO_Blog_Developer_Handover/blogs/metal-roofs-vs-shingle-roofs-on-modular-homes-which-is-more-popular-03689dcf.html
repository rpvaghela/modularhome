Metal Roofs vs Shingle Roofs on Modular Homes: Which Is More Popular?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/metal-roofs-vs-shingle-roofs-on-modular-homes-which-is-more-popular

When it comes to modern modular homes and prefab homes today:

metal roofing has exploded in popularity.

But interestingly:

asphalt shingles are STILL the most common overall roofing choice nationwide.

So the real answer is:

Shingles are still the most common overall

BUT

metal roofs are the FASTEST growing trend in modular homes and prefab housing.

And honestly?
Metal roofing is starting to completely dominate:

  • modern farmhouse modular homes

  • barndominiums

  • modular cabins

  • black modern homes

  • A-frames

  • off-grid cabins

  • Airbnb cabins

Why Shingles Are Still the Most Common Overall

Asphalt shingles remain the most widely used roofing material because they are:

  • cheaper upfront

  • easier to install

  • familiar to contractors

  • available everywhere

  • accepted in almost every subdivision

Most traditional residential homes in America still use:

asphalt shingles.

This includes many:

  • modular homes

  • manufactured homes

  • prefab homes

  • suburban modular developments

Why Metal Roofs Are Exploding in Popularity

Metal roofs became one of the HOTTEST trends in prefab and modular housing because they create:

  • modern aesthetics

  • farmhouse appeal

  • mountain-modern vibes

  • luxury cabin looks

  • rustic-modern architecture

Metal roofs especially dominate:

  • social media

  • Pinterest

  • YouTube

  • Airbnb design trends

because they photograph BEAUTIFULLY.

Why Modular Buyers LOVE Metal Roofs

Modern modular home buyers increasingly want homes that feel:

  • modern

  • luxurious

  • rustic

  • timeless

  • architecturally impressive

Metal roofing aligns PERFECTLY with those trends.

Especially when paired with:

  • black siding

  • cedar accents

  • white farmhouse exteriors

  • giant windows

  • modern porches

Metal Roofs Became the “Modern Farmhouse” Look

One of the biggest reasons metal roofs exploded was:

the farmhouse trend.

The combination of:

  • white farmhouse siding

  • black metal roofing

  • cedar beams

  • black windows

became one of the MOST copied housing styles in America.

That design trend massively accelerated metal roof popularity.

Why Barndominiums Helped Metal Roofing Grow

Barndominiums also helped push metal roofing mainstream.

People increasingly associated metal roofs with:

  • rural luxury

  • modern farmhouses

  • outdoor lifestyles

  • cabin culture

  • steel homes

That emotional branding helped metal roofs explode nationwide.

Why Airbnb Accelerated Metal Roof Popularity

Airbnb massively accelerated:

  • black cabins

  • metal roofs

  • modern prefab cabins

  • A-frame cabins

because metal roofs create:

visually dramatic architecture.

People scrolling online instantly notice:

  • black metal roofs

  • modern cabins

  • mountain-modern prefab homes

Why Metal Roofs Are So Popular on Modular Cabins

Metal roofs are EXTREMELY popular on:

  • modular cabins

  • prefab cabins

  • Amish built cabins

  • off-grid cabins

  • mountain homes

because buyers love:

  • rustic aesthetics

  • durability

  • low maintenance

  • modern cabin vibes

Why Metal Roofs Are Popular in Snow Regions

Metal roofing performs extremely well in:

  • snowy climates

  • mountain regions

  • cold-weather states

because snow tends to:

  • slide off easier

  • accumulate less heavily

This is one reason metal roofs dominate:

  • cabin country

  • mountain homes

  • rural modular cabins

Why Metal Roofs Are Popular in Hurricane Regions

Metal roofs also became popular in:

  • Florida

  • Gulf Coast states

  • coastal regions

because many buyers believe metal roofing offers:

  • excellent wind performance

  • strong durability

  • long-term storm resistance

when engineered properly. (Mills Siding & Roofing)

Why Metal Roofs Last Longer

One major reason people choose metal:

longevity.

Modern metal roofs often last:

  • 40–70+ years

while asphalt shingles commonly last:

  • 20–30 years depending on climate and installation. (Cobex Construction Group)

That long lifespan strongly appeals to:

  • retirees

  • rural homeowners

  • cabin buyers

  • long-term homeowners

Why Metal Roofs Feel More “Luxury”

Modern buyers increasingly associate metal roofs with:

  • luxury homes

  • mountain-modern architecture

  • custom cabins

  • designer farmhouses

That perception changed EVERYTHING.

Years ago:
metal roofs were mostly associated with:

  • barns

  • agricultural buildings

  • commercial structures

Now they are associated with:

modern luxury rural living.

Why Shingles Still Dominate Suburban Areas

Despite metal roofing growth:

shingles still dominate most suburban housing.

Why?

Because shingles:

  • cost less upfront

  • are accepted by nearly every HOA

  • look more traditional

  • are easier to repair

  • are familiar to builders

This is especially true in:

  • large subdivisions

  • suburban neighborhoods

  • production housing developments

Why Shingles Are Still Attractive

Modern architectural shingles can still look:

  • beautiful

  • upscale

  • traditional

  • high-end

Many buyers still prefer the:

classic residential appearance

of shingles.

Why Cost Matters

One major reason shingles remain dominant:

upfront price.

Metal roofs often cost:

  • significantly more upfront

than asphalt shingles. (METALCON)

That higher cost prevents some buyers from upgrading to metal.

Why Buyers Still Choose Metal Anyway

Many buyers still choose metal because they value:

  • longer lifespan

  • reduced maintenance

  • energy efficiency

  • storm durability

  • aesthetics

Many homeowners believe metal roofs provide:

better long-term value.

Why Standing Seam Roofs Became So Popular

Standing seam metal roofs exploded because they create:

  • clean architectural lines

  • luxury aesthetics

  • modern cabin vibes

  • high-end farmhouse appearance

Standing seam became one of the defining looks of:

modern modular architecture.

Why Black Metal Roofs Took Over

Black metal roofs became incredibly popular because they create:

  • dramatic contrast

  • modern aesthetics

  • timeless curb appeal

especially paired with:

  • white siding

  • cedar accents

  • black windows

This combination now dominates:

  • modular homes

  • prefab cabins

  • barndominiums

  • farmhouse modular homes

Why Noise Concerns Are Often Overblown

Many people worry metal roofs will be:

  • loud during rain

But modern homes with:

  • insulation

  • roof decking

  • proper installation

often reduce sound dramatically.

Some homeowners actually LOVE:

the cabin sound of rain on metal roofs.

Why Energy Efficiency Matters

Metal roofs can help reflect sunlight and reduce heat absorption in hot climates depending on:

  • color

  • coatings

  • roof system design. (Mills Siding & Roofing)

This appeals strongly to:

  • Southern buyers

  • off-grid homeowners

  • energy-conscious buyers

Which Is Better Overall?

Honestly?

both are excellent options.

Choose Metal Roofing If You Want:

  • modern farmhouse aesthetics

  • mountain-modern design

  • long lifespan

  • lower long-term maintenance

  • rustic-modern appeal

  • luxury cabin vibes

Choose Shingles If You Want:
