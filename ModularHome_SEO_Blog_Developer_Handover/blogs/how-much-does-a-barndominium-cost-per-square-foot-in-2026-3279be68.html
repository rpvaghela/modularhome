In 2026, barndominiums can range anywhere from simple affordable shell kits to luxury custom homes costing several hundred dollars per square foot.

This guide breaks down realistic barndominium costs, what affects pricing, hidden expenses buyers often forget, and why steel-framed barndominiums continue exploding in popularity.

Average Barndominium Cost Per Square Foot

Most barndominiums fall into one of three major pricing categories:

Basic Shell Kits

Basic shell kits generally range from:

$50–$100 per square foot

These packages often include:

Steel framing
Exterior shell
Roof structure
Structural engineering
Basic shell materials

However, shell kits usually do NOT include:

Foundation
Electrical
Plumbing
HVAC
Drywall
Interior finishes
Cabinets
Flooring
Insulation

Shell kits are popular because they allow buyers to complete interior work separately over time.

Installed Dried-In Shell Pricing

Installed dried-in shells are one of the most popular options today.

Typical pricing ranges from:

$100–$175 per square foot

depending on:

Design complexity
Garage size
Roof structure
Steel framing system
Delivery distance
Window and door packages

Dried-in shell packages commonly include:

Framing
Roof sheathing
Wall sheathing
Weather-resistant shell
Installation labor
Structural engineering

At this stage, the home is protected from weather and ready for interior contractors.

Fully Turnkey Barndominium Pricing

Luxury turnkey barndominiums commonly range from:

$150–$300+ per square foot

depending on the level of customization and finishes.

Luxury features that increase pricing include:

Massive glass walls
Luxury kitchens
Designer bathrooms
Outdoor living areas
Vaulted ceilings
Custom cabinetry
High-end flooring
Large porches
Smart home systems

Luxury barndominiums can easily compete with custom traditional homes in both appearance and price.

Why Barndominiums Can Be More Affordable

Barndominiums became popular partly because they simplify the construction process.

Traditional homes often involve:

Complex rooflines
Heavy framing labor
Multiple subcontractor delays
Long timelines
High labor costs

Barndominiums often reduce these issues through:

Open layouts
Steel framing systems
Large-span construction
Simplified roof structures
Faster shell installation

This can lower both labor and material costs.

What Factors Affect Barndominium Pricing?

Several major things affect cost per square foot.

1. Home Size

Larger homes usually cost more overall but may cost LESS per square foot than smaller homes.

For example:

A 500 sq ft tiny barndo may cost more per square foot than a 2,500 sq ft family barndominium.

2. Garage and Shop Space

Garage space is one of the biggest reasons people build barndominiums.

Popular setups include:

2-car garages
RV garages
Boat storage
Mechanic shops
Woodworking shops
Equipment storage

Large garages increase total square footage but may cost less per square foot than finished living space.

3. Steel vs Wood Framing

Steel-framed barndominiums are becoming increasingly popular because of their durability advantages.

Steel framing offers:

No termites
No warping or twisting
Strong structural integrity
Long-term durability
Modern architectural flexibility

Steel homes may cost slightly more upfront but many buyers believe they offer better long-term value.

4. Interior Finishes

Interior upgrades dramatically affect total project cost.

Luxury features include:

Quartz countertops
Luxury tile showers
Custom cabinets
Smart home systems
Designer kitchens
Floor-to-ceiling glass
Outdoor kitchens

Interior finish selections are often one of the biggest pricing variables.

5. Roof Complexity

Simple roof systems are much cheaper than complex rooflines with:

Dormers
Multiple pitches
Vaulted structures
Large overhangs
Custom framing details

Simple designs are generally the most affordable.

Hidden Costs Most Buyers Forget

Many online barndominium prices only show shell pricing, not total project cost.

Insulation

Interior finishes

Always budget for the FULL project.

Are Barndominiums Cheaper Than Traditional Homes?

In many cases, yes.

Barndominiums can reduce:

Framing complexity
Construction timelines
Labor costs
Material waste

However, highly customized luxury barndominiums can still become expensive projects.

Simple layouts remain the most affordable.

Why Open Floor Plans Help Save Money

Barndominiums are famous for wide open layouts.

This reduces:

Interior framing
Structural complexity
Material waste
Labor time

Open-concept living is also one of the biggest reasons buyers love modern barndo design.

What Is the Cheapest Type of Barndominium?

Generally, the cheapest barndominiums are:

Simple rectangular layouts
Smaller square footage homes
Basic steel shell kits
Minimal roof complexity designs
Open-concept floor plans

The more complex and customized the design becomes, the higher the cost usually gets.
