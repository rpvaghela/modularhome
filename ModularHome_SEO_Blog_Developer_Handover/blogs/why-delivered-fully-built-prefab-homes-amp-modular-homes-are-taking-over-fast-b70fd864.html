What Is a Delivered Fully Built Modular or Prefab Home?

A delivered modular or prefab home is:

a home largely constructed inside a factory

before being:

  • transported to the property

  • craned onto the foundation

  • connected onsite

Many modular homes arrive:

  • 70–90% complete

depending on the project.

Some already include:

  • kitchens

  • bathrooms

  • flooring

  • cabinets

  • electrical

  • plumbing

  • drywall

  • lighting

  • interior finishes

This dramatically reduces:

onsite construction time.
