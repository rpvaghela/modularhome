What Is a Modular ADU Again?

A modular ADU is:

a factory-built accessory dwelling unit

installed on the same property as a primary home.

ADUs are commonly used for:

  • aging parents

  • adult children

  • rental income

  • Airbnb units

  • guest homes

  • home offices

  • downsizing

Modern modular ADUs can include:

  • full kitchens

  • bathrooms

  • bedrooms

  • luxury finishes

  • modern farmhouse designs

  • black modern aesthetics

  • giant windows

Many look like:

beautiful small homes —

NOT backyard sheds.
