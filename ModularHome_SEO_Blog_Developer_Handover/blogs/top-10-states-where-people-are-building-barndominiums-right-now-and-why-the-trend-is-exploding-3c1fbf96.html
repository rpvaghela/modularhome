Top 10 States Where People Are Building Barndominiums Right Now — And Why the Trend Is Exploding

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/top-10-states-where-people-are-building-barndominiums-right-now-and-why-the-trend-is-exploding

Barndominiums are no longer just a Texas trend. Across America, more people are choosing prefab barndominiums, modular barndos, steel home kits, and modern barn-style homes because they offer affordability, flexibility, durability, and faster build times compared to traditional stick-built homes.

At ModularHome.com, we’ve seen massive growth in demand for prefab barndominiums nationwide as homeowners search for affordable housing alternatives, rural living opportunities, Airbnb investments, vacation homes, and multi-purpose properties.

But which states are seeing the biggest barndominium boom?

Here are the Top 10 states where people are placing barndominiums right now — and why these states are dominating the barndo movement.

1. Texas

Why Texas Is the Barndominium Capital of America

Texas remains the undisputed king of barndominiums.

Why?

Because Texas offers:

  • Huge amounts of rural land

  • Lower zoning restrictions in many counties

  • Strong agricultural culture

  • Lower cost per acre compared to coastal states

  • Massive demand for affordable housing

  • Favorable climate for steel buildings

Texas homeowners love the flexibility of barndos because they can combine:

  • living space

  • garages

  • workshops

  • RV storage

  • horse barns

  • home businesses

…all under one roof.

Searches for:

  • Texas barndominiums

  • prefab barndominiums Texas

  • barndominium kits Texas

  • modern barndos Texas

continue exploding every year.

2. Tennessee

Why Tennessee Is Becoming a Barndominium Hotspot

Tennessee has quickly become one of the hottest states for prefab barndominiums because of:

  • mountain land

  • rural acreage

  • lower property taxes

  • huge migration growth

  • strong Airbnb cabin demand

Areas around:

  • Gatlinburg

  • Pigeon Forge

  • Knoxville

  • Nashville outskirts

…have become major targets for modern barndo builds.

People love Tennessee barndominiums for:

  • vacation rentals

  • mountain homes

  • retirement living

  • homesteading

Modern rustic barndos fit perfectly into Tennessee’s scenery and culture.

3. Florida

Why Florida Barndominiums Are Exploding

Florida’s housing market has pushed many buyers toward steel barndominiums and prefab homes as affordable alternatives.

Florida buyers love:

  • hurricane-resistant steel construction

  • open layouts

  • energy efficiency

  • large garage spaces

  • fast build timelines

Popular uses include:

  • RV storage homes

  • workshop homes

  • retirement homes

  • rural homesteads

Barndominiums are especially growing in:

  • Central Florida

  • North Florida

  • rural Gulf Coast areas

4. Oklahoma

Why Oklahoma Loves Barndominiums

Oklahoma has become one of the easiest states for barndominium growth because land remains relatively affordable and rural development is common.

Oklahoma buyers love:

  • steel building durability

  • tornado-resistant structures

  • lower building costs

  • agricultural flexibility

Many Oklahoma homeowners use barndos as:

  • ranch homes

  • horse properties

  • workshop homes

  • hunting cabins

5. Kentucky

Why Kentucky Is Perfect for Barndominiums

Kentucky is quietly becoming a massive barndominium state because it offers:

  • rolling farmland

  • affordable acreage

  • strong rural communities

  • growing prefab housing demand

Kentucky buyers often want:

  • rustic modern designs

  • wraparound porches

  • workshop garages

  • hunting property cabins

The affordability of land makes larger barndominium projects much more realistic than in expensive housing markets.

6. Georgia

Why Georgia Buyers Love Barndos

Georgia combines:

  • strong population growth

  • rural land availability

  • warm climate

  • rising traditional home prices

…which makes prefab barndominiums extremely attractive.

Georgia barndo buyers are often looking for:

  • modern farmhouse aesthetics

  • Airbnb properties

  • family compounds

  • multigenerational housing

North Georgia mountain barndominiums are especially popular right now.

7. North Carolina

Why North Carolina Is Seeing Massive Barndo Growth

North Carolina’s combination of:

  • mountain regions

  • farmland

  • lake properties

  • retirement migration

…has fueled barndominium demand.

Popular uses include:

  • mountain cabins

  • Airbnb investments

  • retirement homes

  • vacation homes

Steel barndominiums also perform well in North Carolina’s humid climate because of lower long-term maintenance.

8. Missouri

Why Missouri Is a Hidden Barndominium Giant

Missouri offers:

  • inexpensive land

  • low cost of living

  • strong rural infrastructure

  • relaxed building conditions in many counties

This makes Missouri extremely attractive for:

  • homesteaders

  • off-grid living

  • workshop homes

  • affordable family housing

Prefab barndominiums are especially popular in rural Missouri communities.

9. Indiana

Why Indiana Is Growing Fast for Modular Barndos

Indiana’s prefab and modular housing market has grown rapidly thanks to:

  • affordable land

  • central U.S. location

  • strong Amish craftsmanship

  • lower building costs

Many Indiana buyers are choosing barndominiums because they can get:

  • larger homes

  • attached garages

  • shop space

  • faster construction

…for significantly less than traditional homes in suburban markets.

10. Colorado

Why Colorado Buyers Are Turning to Barndominiums

Colorado’s skyrocketing home prices have made prefab barndominiums and modular homes increasingly attractive.

Colorado buyers love:

  • modern mountain aesthetics

  • energy efficiency

  • open-concept layouts

  • large glass windows

  • workshop and gear storage

Barndos work especially well for:

  • mountain retreats

  • ski properties

  • outdoor lifestyle homes

Modern steel-and-wood barndominium designs fit perfectly into Colorado’s luxury rural market.
