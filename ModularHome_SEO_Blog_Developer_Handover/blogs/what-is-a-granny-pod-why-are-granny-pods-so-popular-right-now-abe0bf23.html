What Is a Granny Pod?

A granny pod is a small secondary home placed on the same property as a primary house.

They are commonly used for:

  • aging parents

  • grandparents

  • family members

  • guest housing

  • caregivers

  • rental units

Granny pods are essentially a form of:

ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit).

They are often:

  • detached backyard homes

  • prefab cottages

  • modular tiny homes

  • small guest houses

Most granny pods typically include:

  • a bedroom

  • bathroom

  • kitchenette

  • living area

  • private entrance

Some are very simple.
Others are surprisingly luxurious.

Granny pods offer a much more affordable alternative for many families.
