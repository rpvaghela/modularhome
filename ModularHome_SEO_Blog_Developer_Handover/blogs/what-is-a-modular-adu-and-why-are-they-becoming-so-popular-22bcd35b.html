What Does ADU Mean?

ADU stands for:

Accessory Dwelling Unit.

An ADU is basically:

a smaller secondary home

placed on the same property as a primary house.

ADUs are often used for:

  • aging parents

  • adult children

  • guests

  • rental income

  • caretakers

  • home offices

  • downsizing

What Is a Modular ADU?

A modular ADU is:

a factory-built ADU

constructed inside a controlled factory environment and then:

  • delivered to the property

  • craned into place

  • installed onsite

Modern modular ADUs can include:

  • kitchens

  • bathrooms

  • bedrooms

  • living rooms

  • laundry areas

  • porches

  • luxury finishes

Many look like:

beautiful small homes —

NOT backyard sheds.
