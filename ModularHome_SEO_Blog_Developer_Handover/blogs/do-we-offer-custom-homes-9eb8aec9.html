Important: We Do NOT Offer Full Turnkey Custom Homes

For our custom steel house kits and barndominium builds:

dry-in is the furthest level of completion we offer.

We do NOT offer:

  • full turnkey custom homes

  • complete interior finishing

  • fully move-in-ready custom steel homes

Our role is focused on helping you get your custom home:

engineered,

fabricated,

installed,

and

professionally dried in.

After that, you or your contractor would complete:

  • plumbing

  • electrical

  • insulation

  • drywall

  • flooring

  • cabinetry

  • interior finishes

Our 4 Steel House Kit & Barndominium Kit Options

Option 1 — Steel Framing Kit Only

We fabricate and ship your custom engineered steel framing kit, and you install it yourself.

Perfect for:

  • contractors

  • owner-builders

  • DIY customers

Includes:

  • steel framing package

  • stamped engineered drawings for your local code requirements

Option 2 — Steel Framing Kit + ZIP System Panels

We provide:

  • the steel framing package

  • ZIP System wall and roof panels

so you can install the structure yourself and get it dried in faster.

Includes:

  • engineered steel framing

  • ZIP panels

  • stamped engineered drawings

Option 3 — Steel Framing Kit + Professional Installation

We fabricate the steel framing package and our crew comes onsite to professionally install the structure on your prepared foundation.

Includes:

  • steel framing package

  • professional installation

  • stamped engineered drawings

Option 4 — Our Most Popular Option

This is our most requested package.

We:

  • fabricate your steel framing kit

  • provide ZIP System wall and roof panels

  • professionally install the structure

  • dry in the shell

  • install your exterior windows and doors

(as long as your windows and doors are onsite when our install crew arrives).

You then finish the interior at your own pace.

Includes:

  • steel framing kit

  • ZIP System panels

  • professional installation

  • dried-in shell

  • exterior window and door installation

  • stamped engineered drawings

You finish:

  • plumbing

  • electrical

  • insulation

  • drywall

  • flooring

  • cabinetry

  • final interior finishes

from there.
