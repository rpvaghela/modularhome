Full kitchens
Bathrooms
Bedrooms
Living areas
Outdoor spaces

Many buyers view park models as a way to enjoy homeownership, vacation property, or rental income without spending hundreds of thousands of dollars on a large traditional home.

Most park models are:

Modern park models often include:

  • bathrooms

  • lofts

  • bedrooms

  • living rooms

  • porches
