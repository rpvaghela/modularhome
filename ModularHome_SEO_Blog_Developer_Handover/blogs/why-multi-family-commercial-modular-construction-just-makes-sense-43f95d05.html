What Is Multi-Family Modular Construction?

Multi-family modular construction is a building process where apartment units or sections of buildings are constructed off-site inside controlled production facilities and then transported to the property for final installation.

These modules may include:

  • finished apartment units

  • kitchens

  • bathrooms

  • hallways

  • utility systems

  • interior finishes

Once onsite, the modules are:

  • craned into place

  • connected structurally

  • tied into utilities

  • completed externally

The finished buildings can look nearly identical to traditional apartment developments.

For developers:

faster completion = faster revenue.
