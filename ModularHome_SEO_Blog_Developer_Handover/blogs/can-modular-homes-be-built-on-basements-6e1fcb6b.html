Can Modular Homes Be Built on Basements?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/can-modular-homes-be-built-on-basements

One of the MOST common questions people ask about modular homes is:

“Can a modular home go on a basement?”

And the answer is:

YES — absolutely.

In fact,
many modular homes today are installed on:

  • full basements

  • walkout basements

  • crawl spaces

  • slab foundations

just like traditional stick-built homes.

Modern modular homes are:

permanent residential structures

and they can be engineered to work with many different foundation systems depending on:

  • terrain

  • climate

  • budget

  • lifestyle needs

  • local building codes

Modular Homes Are NOT “Trailer Homes”

This is VERY important.

Many people still incorrectly assume modular homes are:

  • temporary structures

  • mobile homes

  • homes sitting on steel chassis

That is NOT how modern modular homes work.

Today’s modular homes are built to:

the same IRC and local county building codes

as traditional stick-built homes.

They are designed to become:

permanent residential homes.

What Is a Modular Home Basement?

A modular basement works very similarly to a traditional basement.

The basement foundation is built onsite first.

Then:

  • the modular sections are delivered

  • craned into place

  • permanently installed on top of the basement foundation

Once complete,
the home functions like:

a normal permanent house.

What Is a Walkout Basement?

A walkout basement is:

a basement built into sloped terrain

allowing one side to open directly outside.

These are EXTREMELY popular for:

  • mountain homes

  • lake homes

  • cabin property

  • hillside lots

Modular homes work VERY well with:

walkout basement designs.
