Are Barndominiums Cheaper to Build? The Complete Cost Breakdown

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/are-barndominiums-cheaper-to-build-the-complete-cost-breakdown

One of the biggest questions people search on Google about barndominiums is:

“Are barndominiums actually cheaper to build than traditional homes?”

The answer is:

In many cases, yes — but it depends heavily on the size, design, materials, location, and level of finish.

Some simple barndominiums can cost significantly less than traditional stick-built homes, while luxury custom barndos can become just as expensive — or even more expensive — than a traditional house.

In this complete guide, we’ll break down exactly why barndominiums can be cheaper, what affects pricing, hidden costs buyers forget about, and why steel-framed barndos are becoming one of the fastest-growing housing categories in America.

Why Barndominiums Can Be Cheaper to Build

Barndominiums became popular largely because they can simplify the construction process.

Traditional homes often involve:

Complex framing
Multiple roof lines
Long construction timelines
High labor costs
Heavy subcontractor coordination
Large material waste

Barndominiums often reduce many of these issues through:

Open layouts
Simplified structures
Steel framing systems
Prefab components
Faster shell installation

This can lower both labor and timeline costs.

How Much Does a Barndominium Cost?

This is one of the most searched questions online.

In 2026, barndominium pricing commonly falls into several categories.

Basic Shell Kit Pricing

Basic steel shell kits may range from:

$50–$100 per square foot

These packages often include:

Steel framing
Exterior shell
Roof system
Structural components
Engineered plans

However, they usually do NOT include:

Electrical
Plumbing
HVAC
Drywall
Interior finishes
Foundation
Utility hookups

Installed Dried-In Shell Pricing

Many buyers choose installed shell packages.

These may range from:

$100–$175 per square foot

depending on:

Design complexity
Roof structure
Garage size
Steel framing system
Delivery distance

These packages often include:

Framing
Roof sheathing
Wall sheathing
Weather-resistant shell
Installation labor

The structure is left dried-in and ready for interior trades.

Turnkey Barndominium Pricing

Luxury turnkey barndominiums may range from:

$150–$300+ per square foot

depending on:

Luxury finishes
Glass walls
Custom kitchens
Garage size
Architecture
High-end bathrooms
Outdoor living spaces

Large luxury barndominiums can still become expensive projects.

Why Open Floor Plans Reduce Cost

One major reason barndominiums can save money is because of their open design.

Barndos typically use:

Fewer interior load-bearing walls
Simpler framing layouts
Large-span structures
Simplified roof systems

This can reduce:

Labor time
Framing complexity
Material waste

Open floor plans are also one of the biggest lifestyle advantages buyers love.

Steel-Framed Barndominiums Are Growing Fast

Steel-framed barndominiums have become extremely popular because of their durability and structural advantages.

Steel framing offers:

No termites
No warping or twisting
Long-term durability
Strong structural integrity
Modern architectural flexibility

Many buyers believe steel-framed homes offer better long-term value than traditional wood framing.

Why Many Buyers Build Shells First

A growing trend is buying a dried-in shell first and finishing the interior over time.

This allows buyers to:

Lock in the structure
Reduce upfront costs
Spread expenses over time
DIY portions of the project

Many barndominium owners complete:

Drywall
Flooring
Cabinets
Interior trim
Paint
Bathrooms

in phases.
