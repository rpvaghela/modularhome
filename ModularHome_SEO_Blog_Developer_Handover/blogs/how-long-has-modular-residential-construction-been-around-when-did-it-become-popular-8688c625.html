How Long Has Modular Residential Construction Been Around? When Did It Become Popular?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/how-long-has-modular-residential-construction-been-around-when-did-it-become-popular

Many people assume modular homes are a brand-new idea because the industry has exploded online recently.

But the truth is:

modular residential construction has been around for well over 100 years.

What HAS changed dramatically is:

  • technology

  • design quality

  • engineering

  • public perception

  • speed of production

  • social media exposure

Modern modular homes today are VERY different from the older prefab homes many people imagine.

And right now, modular construction is entering one of the biggest growth periods in its history.

Modular Construction Actually Dates Back to the Early 1900s

Prefab and modular-style housing concepts began gaining attention in the:

late 1800s and early 1900s.

One of the earliest major examples was:

Sears Catalog Homes.

Between roughly:

  • 1908
    and

  • 1940

Sears sold thousands of mail-order homes across America.

Buyers could order:

  • home kits

  • building materials

  • precut lumber

  • plans

which were shipped by rail and assembled onsite.

While not exactly the same as modern modular construction, these homes helped introduce Americans to:

factory-assisted housing production.

Why Early Prefab Housing Grew

Early prefab systems became popular because they helped:

  • reduce labor

  • simplify construction

  • accelerate homebuilding

  • support growing populations

America was expanding rapidly, and faster housing solutions were needed.

Modular Construction Grew After World War II

One of the biggest growth periods for prefab and modular housing happened after:

World War II.

America suddenly faced:

  • massive housing shortages

  • returning veterans

  • suburban expansion

  • rapidly growing families

The country needed housing FAST.

Factory-built and modular approaches became increasingly attractive because they helped:

  • accelerate production

  • standardize housing

  • lower construction timelines

This period helped establish many of the early prefab and modular systems still influencing the industry today.

Why Manufactured Homes Changed Public Perception

During the mid-to-late 1900s, many people began confusing:

  • modular homes
    with

  • mobile homes
    or

  • manufactured housing.

That confusion affected public perception for decades.

But there is a major difference:

modular homes are built to local/state building codes similar to traditional site-built homes.

Modern modular homes are NOT the same thing as old mobile homes.

The Modern Modular Revolution Started in the 1990s and 2000s

The modern modular industry really began evolving heavily during:

  • the 1990s

  • early 2000s

because:

  • engineering improved

  • factories improved

  • transportation logistics improved

  • building materials improved

  • design quality improved

At the same time:

  • labor shortages started increasing

  • construction costs started rising

  • developers began searching for more efficient systems

This pushed modular construction forward rapidly.

Modular Became MUCH More Popular in the 2010s

The modular industry REALLY started accelerating during:

the 2010s.

Several major trends helped fuel growth:

1. Tiny Homes Went Viral

Tiny homes exploded across:

  • YouTube

  • HGTV

  • Pinterest

  • Instagram

This helped normalize:

  • smaller homes

  • prefab living

  • efficient housing

2. Airbnb Changed Everything

Airbnb massively increased demand for:

  • cabins

  • prefab homes

  • modular retreats

  • glamping units

  • tiny homes

Modular construction became attractive because it allowed investors to:

  • build faster

  • scale cabins quickly

  • create rental inventory faster

3. Social Media Made Modern Modular Homes Look Cool

Older modular homes often had outdated stereotypes.

But modern modular homes now featured:

  • black modern exteriors

  • giant windows

  • luxury interiors

  • farmhouse aesthetics

  • Scandinavian design

  • steel framing

Social media completely changed public perception.

4. Construction Costs Exploded

Traditional construction became increasingly difficult because of:

  • labor shortages

  • material inflation

  • subcontractor delays

  • weather issues

  • longer timelines

Modular systems became more attractive because they offered:

  • faster timelines

  • factory production

  • predictable scheduling

The Pandemic Accelerated Modular Growth MASSIVELY

COVID changed housing dramatically.

People suddenly wanted:

  • rural property

  • faster housing solutions

  • cabins

  • backyard ADUs

  • home offices

  • vacation rentals

  • remote living

At the same time:

  • labor shortages worsened

  • material shortages increased

  • traditional construction slowed down

This massively accelerated interest in:

  • prefab homes

  • modular homes

  • steel house kits

  • ADUs

  • tiny homes

The industry is booming today because modular construction aligns perfectly with major modern trends:

Faster Construction

People want housing faster.

Housing Shortages

America desperately needs more housing.

Labor Problems

Construction labor shortages continue worsening.

Airbnb Growth

Cabins and prefab rentals perform extremely well.

People increasingly want land and outdoor lifestyles.

Modern Design

Today’s modular homes actually look amazing.

Commercial modular construction is now exploding in:

because developers want:

  • faster occupancy

  • speed

Modular construction aligns perfectly with these values.

Final Thoughts: How Long Has Modular Construction Been Around?

Modular residential construction has existed in various forms for:

over 100 years.

But the industry truly began accelerating during:

  • the 1990s

thanks to:

  • modern design
