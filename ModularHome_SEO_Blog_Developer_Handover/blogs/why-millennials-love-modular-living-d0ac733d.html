Who Are Millennials?

Millennials are generally people born between:

1981–1996.

This generation came of age during:

  • rising housing costs

  • student debt growth

  • economic uncertainty

  • inflation

  • skyrocketing rent prices

As a result, Millennials often approach housing VERY differently than older generations.

more achievable.
