What Are the Most Popular Modular Home Sizes and Bedroom/Bathroom Combos?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/what-are-the-most-popular-modular-home-sizes-and-bedroom-bathroom-combos

One of the MOST common questions buyers ask when shopping for modular homes is:

“What size modular home do most people actually buy?”

The answer depends on:

  • budget

  • family size

  • land

  • lifestyle

  • retirement goals

  • Airbnb use

  • rural vs suburban living

But across America, several modular home layouts clearly dominate the market.

The MOST popular modular home sizes today are typically:

1. 3 Bed / 2 Bath

2. 2 Bed / 2 Bath

3. 4 Bed / 2 Bath

4. 1 Bed / 1 Bath ADU & Tiny Homes

And the sweet spot for most modular home buyers tends to fall between:

1,200–2,200 square feet.

That range balances:

  • affordability

  • livability

  • energy efficiency

  • resale appeal

  • transportation efficiency

  • family functionality

The MOST common modular home layout in America right now is:

3 bedroom, 2 bathroom.

This is considered the:

“universal family floor plan.”

Why?

Because it works for:

  • families

  • couples

  • retirees

  • Airbnb investors

  • remote workers

  • first-time buyers

The most common sizes usually range around:

  • 1,400 sq ft

  • 2,000 sq ft

This range gives buyers:

  • comfortable living space

  • open kitchens

  • larger living rooms

  • primary suites

  • guest rooms

  • office flexibility

without becoming:

  • too expensive

  • too difficult to maintain

  • too energy intensive

Why 3 Bed / 2 Bath Dominates

This layout became dominant because it offers:

  • flexibility

  • strong resale appeal

  • practical family living

  • guest room space

  • work-from-home options

Many buyers use the third bedroom for:
