What Is a House Kit?

A house kit is a pre-designed package of building materials and components shipped to a property for assembly.

Instead of building a house completely from scratch onsite, the kit provides major structural materials already:

  • engineered

  • cut

  • designed

  • organized

depending on the type of kit.

Think of it as:

a simplified building system for constructing a home.

What Comes in a House Kit?

This depends heavily on the company and the type of kit.

Some basic house kits may include:

  • framing materials

  • roof systems

  • exterior sheathing

  • structural components

  • windows

  • doors

  • siding

  • metal roofing

More advanced kits may also include:

  • insulation systems

  • interior framing

  • stair systems

  • porches

  • hardware packages

Some turnkey-style kits may include nearly everything except:

  • foundation

  • utilities

  • final finish work

What Usually Does NOT Come With a House Kit?

This is VERY important.

Many house kits do NOT include:

  • land

  • site work

  • excavation

  • foundations

  • utility hookups

  • plumbing installation

  • electrical installation

  • HVAC installation

  • drywall

  • flooring

  • final finishes

Every kit company is different, which is why buyers should always review:

exactly what is included.

Types of House Kits

These are extremely popular for:

  • rustic aesthetics

  • porches

  • loft spaces

  • cozy layouts

2. Steel House Kits

Steel kits are booming because buyers love:

  • durability

  • modern design

  • lower maintenance

  • faster shell construction

These kits often use:

  • steel framing

  • metal exteriors

  • engineered systems

3. Barndominium Kits

Barndominiums became hugely popular because they combine:

  • open floor plans

  • garages

  • shops

  • residential living

Barndo kits are especially popular in rural areas.

4. Tiny Home Kits

Tiny house kits focus on:

  • affordability

  • compact living

  • DIY construction

  • Airbnb opportunities

5. A-Frame Kits

A-frame house kits exploded because of:

  • Airbnb

  • social media

  • cabin culture

  • mountain living trends

Their iconic triangular design photographs extremely well online.

6. Panelized House Kits

These kits use:

  • pre-built wall panels

  • engineered structural systems

  • factory-cut materials

which can dramatically speed up assembly.

7. SIP House Kits

SIP (Structural Insulated Panel) kits use high-performance insulated wall and roof panels designed for:

  • energy efficiency

  • fast installation

  • modern construction

Are House Kits the Same as Modular Homes?

Not always.

This is a common misunderstanding.

House Kits

House kits usually arrive as:

  • materials

  • panels

  • framing systems

  • structural packages

and are assembled onsite.

Modular Homes

Modular homes are usually:

  • factory-built sections

  • transported as completed modules

  • craned into place onsite

Both are forms of prefab construction —
but they use different delivery methods.
