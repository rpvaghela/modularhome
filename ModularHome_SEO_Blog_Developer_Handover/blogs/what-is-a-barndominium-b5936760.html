What Is a Barndominium?

A barndominium is a residential home designed using the style, structure, or framing system commonly associated with barns, shops, or metal buildings.

The word “barndominium” combines:

Barn + Condominium

Originally, the term referred to simple barn structures converted into living spaces. But modern barndominiums have evolved dramatically and are now some of the most beautiful custom homes being built today.

Modern barndominiums often include:

Luxury kitchens
Massive garages
Open-concept floor plans
Vaulted ceilings
Wraparound porches
Large glass windows
Lofts
Workshops
Outdoor living spaces
Steel framing systems

Today’s barndos can range from simple rustic cabins all the way to luxury modern estates worth hundreds of thousands or even millions of dollars.

What Are Barndominiums Built With?

Modern barndominiums are usually built using one of several construction methods.

Steel-Framed Barndominiums

One of the most popular methods today.

Steel-framed barndos use engineered steel framing systems instead of traditional wood framing.

Advantages include:

Termite resistance
No warping or twisting
Long-term durability
Strong structural integrity
Modern design flexibility

Steel-framed homes are one of the fastest-growing sectors in prefab housing.

Post-Frame Barndominiums

Post-frame construction uses large posts or columns as the main structural system.

This method can create:

Large open spans
Wide garage areas
Workshop space
Open interiors

Traditional Wood-Framed Barndominiums

Some barndominiums are still built using traditional stick-built framing while maintaining the barn-style aesthetic.
