What Is Modular Construction?

Modular construction is a building process where sections of a structure are built off-site in a controlled environment and then transported to the final property for installation.

These sections are called:

  • modules

  • modular units

  • prefabricated sections

Once delivered, the modules are:

  • craned into place

  • connected together

  • finished onsite

Modern modular buildings can be nearly indistinguishable from traditional construction once completed.
