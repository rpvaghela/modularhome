What Is Included in a Turnkey Modular Home Package?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/what-is-included-in-a-turnkey-modular-home-package

One of the MOST common questions people ask when shopping for a modular home is:

“What exactly is included?”

And honestly?
This is VERY important because:

not all modular home packages are the same.

Some companies offer:

  • shell-only homes
    while others offer:

  • partial completion
    or

  • full turnkey modular homes

Understanding the difference can save buyers:

  • money

  • confusion

  • delays

  • unrealistic expectations

What Does “Turnkey” Mean?

In housing,
“turnkey” generally means:

move-in ready.

A true turnkey modular home is typically delivered:

  • fully completed

  • utility connected

  • ready for occupancy

with little or no additional work required by the buyer.

What Is Usually Included in a Turnkey Modular Home?

While every builder and package differs,
many turnkey modular homes commonly include:

  • completed modular home sections

  • delivery to the property

  • crane setting

  • foundation installation coordination

  • exterior completion

  • interior finish work

  • kitchens

  • bathrooms

  • HVAC systems

  • plumbing systems

  • electrical systems

  • flooring

  • cabinets

  • countertops

  • fixtures

  • insulation

  • drywall

  • paint

  • utility hookups

  • final inspections

Why Buyers LOVE Turnkey Modular Homes

Many buyers choose turnkey because they want:

  • simplicity

  • convenience

  • faster move-ins

  • less contractor coordination

  • fewer construction headaches

Instead of managing:

  • multiple subcontractors

  • scheduling

  • inspections

  • material orders

the modular company handles much of the process.

What Is Usually NOT Included?

This surprises many buyers.

Even turnkey packages may NOT always include:

  • land

  • permits

  • excavation

  • septic systems

  • well systems

  • driveway installation

  • landscaping

  • utility company fees

  • impact fees

  • retaining walls

  • special site engineering

Every company structures packages differently.

Why Site Prep Matters MASSIVELY

One of the biggest overlooked costs in modular construction is:

site preparation.

Depending on the property,
site work can include:

  • clearing trees

  • grading

  • excavation

  • foundation work

  • utility trenching

  • drainage systems

  • driveway access

Site conditions greatly affect:

  • timeline

  • budget

  • feasibility

What About the Foundation?

Turnkey modular homes are typically installed on:

  • crawl spaces

  • slab foundations

  • full basements

  • walkout basements

Foundation work may be:

  • included
    OR

  • handled separately

depending on the builder.

Are Appliances Included?

Sometimes:

yes —

sometimes no.

Some turnkey packages include:

  • refrigerators

  • ovens

  • dishwashers

  • microwaves

  • washer/dryer hookups

while others only include:

  • appliance allowances
    or

  • no appliances at all.

Why Clarifying Specifications Matters

This is VERY important.

Buyers should always confirm:

  • what is included

  • what is excluded

  • what is optional

  • what upgrades cost

before assuming a package is:

fully move-in ready.

What Is a “Dry-In” Package?

Many companies today also offer:

dry-in modular packages.

This usually means:

  • the structure is complete

  • exterior shell is weather-tight

  • roof is installed

  • windows and doors are installed

but the buyer completes:

  • interior finishing

  • mechanical systems

  • flooring

  • cabinets

  • paint

  • trim

Why Some Buyers Prefer Dry-In Packages

Dry-in packages are popular because they can:

  • lower upfront costs

  • allow sweat equity

  • provide more customization

  • reduce total project pricing

especially for:

  • rural buyers

  • DIY customers

  • investors

  • owner-builders

Why Modular Construction Simplifies Turnkey Housing

Traditional construction often requires coordinating:

  • framers

  • roofers

  • electricians

  • plumbers

  • drywall crews

  • painters

  • finish carpenters

Factory-built modular systems streamline much of this process because major portions of the home are completed:

before arriving onsite.

“turnkey”

a move-in-ready home solution
