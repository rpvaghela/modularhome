What Is a SIP Panel Home?

SIP stands for:

Structural Insulated Panel.

A SIP panel is basically:

structure + insulation combined into one engineered building panel.

Most SIP panels contain:

  • rigid foam insulation core
    sandwiched between:

  • structural outer layers

These panels are then assembled together onsite to create:

  • walls

  • roofs

  • floors

  • entire home systems

What Does “Panelized” Mean?

Panelized means:

large engineered sections of the home are prefabricated before arriving onsite.

Instead of framing a house:

  • board by board

  • stick by stick

large wall panels arrive ready for assembly.

Think of it like:

a giant engineered puzzle system.
