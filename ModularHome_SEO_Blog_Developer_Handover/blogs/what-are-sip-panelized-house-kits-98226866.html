What Does SIP Mean?

SIP stands for:

Structural Insulated Panel.

A SIP panel is basically:

a giant engineered building panel

that combines:

  • structural framing
    AND

  • insulation

into one integrated system.

These panels are used for:

  • exterior walls

  • roofs

  • floors

depending on the home design.

What Is Inside a SIP Panel?

Most SIP panels consist of:

  • rigid foam insulation in the middle
    sandwiched between:

  • structural sheathing layers

usually:

  • OSB (oriented strand board)

on both sides.

Think of it like:

a giant insulated structural sandwich panel.

What Is a SIP Panelized House Kit?

A SIP panelized house kit is:

a precut engineered building package

where the wall and roof panels are manufactured in a factory and shipped to the homesite ready for assembly.

Instead of framing every wall stick-by-stick onsite,
the home arrives as:

  • large wall panels

  • roof sections

  • engineered structural pieces

that are assembled together onsite.

Why SIP Homes Are Extremely Energy Efficient

One of the BIGGEST advantages of SIP construction is:

Traditional stick framing creates:

  • thermal breaks

  • insulation gaps

  • air leakage points

SIP panels dramatically reduce many of these issues because insulation remains:

continuous throughout the panel system. (energy.gov)

This can create:

  • tighter homes

  • lower utility costs

  • better temperature control

  • improved HVAC efficiency

What Does “Panelized” Mean?

Panelized simply means:

the home arrives in large prebuilt sections.

Instead of delivering:

  • fully completed modules like modular homes

SIP panelized systems deliver:

  • wall panels

  • roof panels

  • structural sections

that are assembled onsite.

SIP vs Modular Homes

This confuses many buyers.

SIP = Panel Building System

Modular = Fully Built Room Sections

Modular homes arrive:

  • mostly complete

  • often with kitchens and bathrooms already installed

SIP homes arrive more like:

  • giant engineered puzzle pieces.
