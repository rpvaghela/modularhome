Can You Finance a Modular Home Like a Normal House?

Source: https://modularhome.com/blogs/modular-homes/can-you-finance-a-modular-home-like-a-normal-house

One of the BIGGEST misconceptions about modular homes is people assume:

“Banks won’t finance them.”

But honestly?
Modern modular homes today are commonly financed:

just like traditional stick-built houses.

In fact,
as modular construction has become more mainstream,
more banks, lenders, and mortgage companies now fully recognize modular homes as:

  • permanent residential housing

  • appreciating real estate

  • code-built homes

  • long-term residential investments

And that is a HUGE reason modular homes are exploding in popularity nationwide.

YES — Modular Homes Can Usually Be Financed Like Traditional Homes

Modern modular homes are commonly financed using:

  • conventional mortgages

  • construction loans

  • FHA loans

  • VA loans

  • USDA loans

  • land/home packages

  • jumbo financing in some cases

depending on:

  • the lender

  • the project

  • the borrower

  • the land situation

Why Banks Are More Comfortable With Modular Homes Today

Years ago,
many lenders misunderstood modular homes and confused them with:

  • mobile homes

  • manufactured homes

  • temporary structures

That perception changed dramatically.

Modern modular homes are now widely recognized as:

permanent residential homes built to residential code.

Modular Homes Are Built to IRC Code

This is VERY important.

Modern modular homes are built to:

the same IRC and local county building codes

as traditional site-built homes.

IRC stands for:

International Residential Code.

That means modular homes must still meet:

  • structural requirements

  • insulation requirements

  • electrical codes

  • plumbing codes

  • energy standards

  • wind requirements

  • snow-load requirements

just like stick-built houses.

Why This Matters to Banks

Banks care about:

  • structural quality

  • long-term durability

  • resale value

  • code compliance

  • appraisal stability

Because modular homes meet residential building standards,
many lenders increasingly view them:

similarly to traditional housing.

Why Appraisers Became More Comfortable With Modular Homes

As modular housing became more common,
appraisers increasingly began using:

  • modular home comparables

  • prefab home market data

  • factory-built housing sales history

This helped modular homes gain:

stronger market acceptance.

Why Modular Homes Often Appraise VERY Well

Modern modular homes today can include:

  • luxury kitchens

  • giant windows

  • farmhouse designs

  • spa bathrooms

  • modern architecture

  • high-end finishes

Many are visually indistinguishable from:

custom stick-built homes.

That helped improve:

  • appraisal confidence

  • lender confidence

  • buyer confidence

Why Permanent Foundations Matter

Most financeable modular homes are installed on:

permanent foundations.

These can include:

  • crawl spaces

  • basements

  • slab foundations

Permanent installation is one of the biggest differences between:

  • modular homes
    and

  • mobile/manufactured homes on chassis systems

What Is a Construction-to-Permanent Loan?

Many modular buyers use:

construction-to-permanent financing.

This means:

  • the lender funds construction
    then

  • converts the project into a long-term mortgage once complete.

This is VERY common in:

  • modular construction

  • custom homes

  • rural property development

Can You Finance Land + Modular Home Together?

YES.

Many buyers combine:

  • land purchase
    and

  • modular home financing

into:

one package loan

depending on the lender and project structure.

many more buyers nationwide.

Banks increasingly realize modular construction helps solve:

  • permanent homes

Modular homes often build faster because:

  • site work
    and

  • factory construction

  • long construction timelines

  • project uncertainty

which lenders often appreciate.

Why Controlled Factory Construction Builds Confidence

Traditional stick-built homes can sit exposed to:

  • rain

  • snow

  • humidity

  • weather delays

for weeks before drying in.

Modular homes are largely built:

indoors in factory-controlled environments.

This helps improve:

  • workflow consistency

  • material protection

  • construction organization

Why Modular Homes Often Appreciate Like Traditional Homes

This surprises many people.

Modern modular homes installed on permanent foundations often appreciate:

similarly to traditional homes

depending on:

  • location

  • land value

  • market demand

  • home quality

The idea that modular homes “always lose value” is:

outdated and often false.

YES — absolutely.

Modern modular homes are commonly financed very similarly to traditional stick-built homes because they are:
