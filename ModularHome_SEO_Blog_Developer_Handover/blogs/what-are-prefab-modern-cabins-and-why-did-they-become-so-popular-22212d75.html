What Is a Prefab Modern Cabin?

A prefab modern cabin is typically:

a factory-built or partially factory-built cabin

designed with:

  • modern architecture

  • open layouts

  • energy-efficient systems

  • minimalist aesthetics

  • indoor-outdoor living

Prefab cabins may include:

  • modular cabins

  • SIP cabins

  • panelized cabins

  • steel cabin kits

  • prefab A-frames

The word “prefab” simply means:

prefabricated.

In other words:
parts of the cabin are built:

  • offsite

  • inside a factory

  • under controlled conditions

before being delivered and assembled onsite.
