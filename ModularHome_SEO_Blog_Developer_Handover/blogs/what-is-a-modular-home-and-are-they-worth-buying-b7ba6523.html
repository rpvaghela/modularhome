What Is a Modular Home?

A modular home is:

a home built inside a factory in large sections called modules.

Those modules are then:

  • transported to the homesite

  • craned onto the foundation

  • permanently installed onsite

Once installed,
the home functions just like a traditional residential house.

Modular Homes Are NOT Mobile Homes

This is VERY important.

Many people still confuse:

  • modular homes
    with

  • mobile homes or manufactured homes.

Modern modular homes are built to:

the SAME IRC and local county building codes

as traditional stick-built homes.

They are:

  • permanent residential homes

  • engineered structures

  • code-compliant houses

NOT:

  • RVs

  • trailers

  • temporary units

What Does IRC Mean?

IRC stands for:

International Residential Code.

This is the same residential building code system used for:

  • traditional site-built homes

  • modular homes

  • many prefab homes

That means modular homes must still meet:

  • structural codes

  • wind codes

  • snow-load requirements

  • insulation standards

  • plumbing codes

  • electrical codes

  • energy requirements

just like traditional construction.

How Are Modular Homes Built?

The modules are constructed:

indoors in a factory-controlled environment.

This is one of the biggest advantages.

Instead of sitting exposed to:

  • rain

  • snow

  • weather

  • humidity

like many stick-built homes,
modular homes are largely protected during construction.

Why Factory Construction Matters

We have all seen traditional homes being framed onsite while:

  • rain hits exposed subfloors

  • framing sits wet

  • weather delays drag projects out

Sometimes homes get rained on repeatedly before the roof is even installed.

Factory-built modular homes avoid much of this exposure because they are built:

under roof indoors.

This helps reduce:

  • weather delays

  • material exposure

  • moisture intrusion risk

  • warped materials

Why Modular Homes Are Often Built VERY Strong

This surprises many people.

Modular homes must survive:

  • transportation

  • highway movement

  • crane lifting

Because of this,
many modular homes require:

  • reinforced framing

  • rigid floor systems

  • stronger structural assemblies

Many modular homes feel:

extremely solid.
