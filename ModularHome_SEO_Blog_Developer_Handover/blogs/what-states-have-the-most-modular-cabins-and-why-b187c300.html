What Is a Modular Cabin?

A modular cabin is:

a factory-built cabin

constructed in sections inside a factory and then:

  • delivered to the property

  • craned onto the foundation

  • permanently installed onsite

Modern modular cabins can include:

  • farmhouse interiors

  • giant windows

  • luxury kitchens

  • fireplaces

  • lofts

  • porches

  • spa bathrooms

  • black modern exteriors

Many now feel more like:

luxury mountain homes

than old rustic hunting cabins.
